Steps today
Enter your step count.

This sets your total for the day — entering a new number replaces the old one, so you can update it as the day goes on. Steps count 42.5 kcal per 1,000.

Auto-sync from Apple Health

Browsers can’t read Apple Health directly — Apple only opens it to native apps. But this page listens for #steps=8500 on its address. If you host this file at a URL, an iOS Shortcut (“Get health sample → Steps today → Open URL”) can push your live step count in one tap or on a schedule. Until then, two taps here does it.
